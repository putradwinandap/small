# Habit Rules

- The MVP has no free-form skip status; a scheduled day is either complete or missed.
- The MVP schedule is daily. Future schedule patterns must preserve the same completion and recovery rules.
- A user may have one active habit until the unlock rule is met.
- Unlock requires at least 14 scheduled days and 10 completions.
- A missed day breaks the streak but never deletes completion history.
- Two consecutive missed days enter recovery mode.
- Three consecutive completions exit recovery mode.
- Recovery completion does not replace the unlock threshold.
- Passing 14 scheduled days with only five completions does not unlock a second habit.
- Editing or archiving a habit does not bypass the unlock rule.
